# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: What is Dental CRM?**
A: Enterprise-grade customer relationship management software specifically designed for dental practices.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Pricing varies by plan. Contact sales for details.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! All data is encrypted, we follow HIPAA guidelines, and use enterprise-grade security.

## Setup

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: Initial setup takes 15-30 minutes. Full configuration depends on your needs.

**Q: Can I import existing patient data?**
A: Yes! Use the CSV import feature in Settings → Import/Export.

**Q: Do I need technical knowledge?**
A: No! The interface is designed to be intuitive for non-technical users.

## Features

**Q: Can I customize pipelines?**
A: Yes! Create unlimited custom pipelines in Settings → Pipelines.

**Q: Does it integrate with my PMS?**
A: Yes! We support bidirectional sync with major PMS systems.

**Q: Can I send automated campaigns?**
A: Yes! Create email, SMS, and WhatsApp campaigns with automation.

## Usage

**Q: How many team members can I add?**
A: Unlimited on Enterprise plan. Check your plan details.

**Q: Can I use it on mobile?**
A: Yes! Fully responsive and works on all devices.

**Q: Is there an app?**
A: It's a web app (PWA) that works like a native app.

## Billing

**Q: How does billing work?**
A: Monthly or annual subscriptions. See Settings → Billing.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes! Cancel anytime from Settings → Billing.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit cards, debit cards via Stripe.

## Technical

**Q: Where is data stored?**
A: Secure cloud database (Supabase/PostgreSQL).

**Q: Do you have an API?**
A: Yes! See API_DOCUMENTATION.md for details.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes! Settings → Privacy → Export All Data.

## Support

**Q: How do I get help?**
A: Email support@dentalcrm.com or use in-app chat.

**Q: What are support hours?**
A: 24/7 for Enterprise, business hours for other plans.

**Q: Do you offer training?**
A: Yes! Free onboarding and training videos included.
