## 🧠 **HOW THE AI SCORING WORKS:**

### **Likelihood Score Calculation:**

**Base Score:** 50%

**Positive Factors (+):**
- Positive sentiment: +20%
- Many interactions (>5): +10%
- Recent contact (<3 days): +15%
- High urgency: +10%

**Negative Factors (-):**
- Negative sentiment: -20%
- Cold lead (>14 days): -20%
- Low engagement (<2 talks): -10%

**Result:** 0-100% likelihood score

---

### **Health Status:**
```
90-100% → Excellent 🟢
65-89%  → Good 🔵
40-64%  → Fair 🟡
20-39%  → Poor 🟠
0-19%   → Critical 🔴
```

---

### **Sentiment Analysis:**
Analyzes AI artifacts from conversations:
- Keywords: "interested", "enthusiastic" → Positive
- Keywords: "hesitant", "concerned" → Negative
- Neutral if balanced

---

### **Next Best Action Logic:**
```
If cold (>14 days) → "🚨 Urgent: Re-engage immediately"
If negative sentiment → "💬 Address concerns"
If positive sentiment → "✅ Send treatment plan"
If high urgency → "⚡ Schedule ASAP"
If low engagement → "📧 Send educational material"
```
