# Understanding Your Marketing Audit Scores

## Complete Guide to Score Interpretation

---

## Composite Score (0-100)

Your **overall marketing health score** is a weighted average of 5 categories:

```
Composite = (Technical × 25%) + (Local × 30%) + (Content × 20%) + 
            (Analytics × 15%) + (Conversion × 10%)
```

### Score Ranges:
- **90-100:** Excellent - Top 10% of practices
- **80-89:** Very Good - Above average
- **70-79:** Good - Solid foundation
- **60-69:** Fair - Room for improvement
- **40-59:** Needs Work - Significant gaps
- **0-39:** Poor - Major issues to address

---

## Category Scores Explained

### 1. Technical SEO (Weight: 25%)

**What it measures:**
- Core Web Vitals (page speed metrics)
- Lighthouse scores (performance, accessibility, SEO)
- Indexation status (are your pages in Google?)
- Mobile-friendliness
- HTTPS security
- XML sitemap presence

**Why it matters:**
- Google ranks faster sites higher
- Poor technical SEO = invisible in search
- Directly affects patient experience

**How to improve:**
- Fix Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS)
- Resolve indexation errors in Search Console
- Enable HTTPS if not already
- Add/update XML sitemap
- Improve Lighthouse scores

---

### 2. Local Presence (Weight: 30%)

**What it measures:**
- Google Business Profile completeness
- Review count and rating
- Review velocity (per month)
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- Citation count (directory listings)
- Local pack presence

**Why it matters:**
- 30% weight = most important for dental practices
- Local searches = high-intent patients
- Reviews drive trust and bookings

**How to improve:**
- Systematically request reviews (aim for 15-25/month)
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- Complete your Google Business Profile 100%
- Get listed in 50+ dental directories
- Ensure NAP is identical everywhere

---

### 3. Content & Authority (Weight: 20%)

**What it measures:**
- Number of indexed pages
- Domain authority score (Phase 3)
- Backlink quality and quantity (Phase 3)
- Content freshness
- Organic keyword rankings (Phase 3)

**Why it matters:**
- More quality content = rank for more keywords
- Backlinks = authority and trust signals
- Authority drives rankings across all keywords

**How to improve:**
- Create 50-100 pages of quality content
- Write blog posts on dental topics
- Build high-quality backlinks (guest posts, directories)
- Update content regularly
- Disavow toxic backlinks

---

### 4. Analytics Hygiene (Weight: 15%)

**What it measures:**
- Google Analytics 4 setup
- Search Console connection
- Custom event tracking
- UTM parameter usage (campaign tagging)
- Cookie consent compliance
- Privacy policy presence

**Why it matters:**
- Can't improve what you don't measure
- Attribution shows which marketing works
- Compliance protects you legally

**How to improve:**
- Connect GA4 if not already
- Connect Search Console
- Set up 5-10 custom events (form submits, phone clicks)
- Tag all campaigns with UTM parameters (90%+ target)
- Add cookie consent banner
- Create privacy policy page

---

### 5. Conversion UX (Weight: 10%)

**What it measures:**
- Online booking widget presence
- Click-to-call on mobile
- Phone number visibility
- Contact form accessibility
- Mobile responsiveness
- Trust signals (reviews widget, certifications)
- CTA placement

**Why it matters:**
- Traffic is worthless without conversions
- Every friction point loses patients
- Mobile UX especially critical (60% of traffic)

**How to improve:**
- Add online booking (Calendly, Acuity, etc.)
- Make phone clickable on mobile
- Put phone number in header
- Simplify contact form (4 fields max)
- Add reviews widget to homepage
- Display certifications/memberships

## Benchmarking Metrics

### Percentile Rank
**What it is:** What percentage of peers you outperform

- **90th percentile:** You beat 90% of local practices (top 10%)
- **75th percentile:** You beat 75% (top quartile)
- **50th percentile:** Median (average)
- **25th percentile:** Bottom quartile

**Example:** If you're 65th percentile, you're better than 65% of local dental practices.

### Your Rank
**What it is:** Your absolute position (1st, 2nd, 3rd...)

- **#1-3:** You're a top performer
- **#4-10:** Above average
- **#11-20:** Average to below average

### Gap to Median
**What it is:** Points difference vs the middle practice

- **+10 points:** You're 10 points better than median (strong position)
- **-5 points:** You're 5 points below median (room to improve)

### Gap to Top 3
**What it is:** Points difference vs average of top 3 practices

- **-15 points:** You need 15 points to match top performers
- **+5 points:** You're already beating the top 3!

## Priority Scores (Recommendations)

Each recommendation has a priority score (0-100) based on:

**Formula:** `((Impact + Effort) / 2) × Confidence`

- **Impact:** high=90, medium=60, low=30
- **Effort:** low=100, medium=60, high=30
- **Confidence:** high=1.0, medium=0.8, low=0.6

**Priority Ranges:**
- **90-100:** Critical - Do these first
- **80-89:** High priority - Do soon
- **70-79:** Medium priority - Important but not urgent
- **Below 70:** Low priority - Do if time permits

**Example:**
- High impact + Low effort + High confidence = 95 (do immediately!)
- High impact + High effort + Medium confidence = 72 (plan carefully)
- Low impact + High effort + Low confidence = 30 (skip or defer)

## How Scores Change Over Time

### What Causes Scores to Improve:
- ✅ Fixing Core Web Vitals
- ✅ Getting more reviews
- ✅ Building backlinks
- ✅ Adding content
- ✅ Improving UX

### What Causes Scores to Drop:
- ❌ Site speed degradation
- ❌ Decline in review velocity
- ❌ Lost backlinks
- ❌ Indexation errors
- ❌ Broken tracking

### Typical Improvement Timelines:
- **Quick wins (1 week):** Add sitemap, enable HTTPS, fix broken links
- **Medium term (1 month):** Improve Core Web Vitals, get 20-30 reviews
- **Long term (3-6 months):** Build 50+ backlinks, create 50+ pages

- Reach 60+ overall score

- Raise composite score by additional 10 points
- Get to 70+ overall score
- Reach top quartile (75th percentile)

**Q3-Q4 (Months 7-12):**
- Reach 80+ overall score
- Reach top 10% (90th percentile)
