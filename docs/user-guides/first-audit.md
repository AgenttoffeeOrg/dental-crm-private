# How to Run Your First Marketing Audit

## Quick Start Guide

**Time Required:** 5 minutes to start, 2-3 minutes for audit to complete

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Marketing Audit

1. Open your Dental CRM dashboard
2. Look for **"Marketing Audit"** in the left sidebar (with a "New" badge)
3. Click to open the Marketing Audit module

---

## Step 2: Run Your First Audit

1. You'll see a welcome screen explaining the audit
2. Click the large **"Run Your First Audit"** button
3. The system will start analyzing your practice's online presence

**What happens next:**
- The audit runs automatically for 2-3 minutes
- You can stay on the page or navigate away
- You'll get a notification when it's complete

---

## Step 3: Review Your Results

Once the audit completes, you'll see:

### Your Marketing Health Score (0-100)
- **90-100:** Excellent - You're doing great!
- **80-89:** Very Good - Minor improvements possible
- **70-79:** Good - Some areas to work on
- **60-69:** Fair - Notable gaps to address
- **40-59:** Needs Work - Significant improvements needed
- **0-39:** Poor - Major issues to fix

### 5 Category Scores
1. **Technical SEO (25%)** - Site speed, Core Web Vitals, indexation
2. **Local Presence (30%)** - Google Business Profile, reviews
3. **Content & Authority (20%)** - Website content, backlinks
4. **Analytics (15%)** - GA4, Search Console setup
5. **Conversion UX (10%)** - Booking widgets, CTAs, mobile experience

### Competitor Benchmarking
- Your rank vs nearby dental practices
- Percentile position (e.g., "You're 65th percentile")
- Gaps to top performers

### Recommendations
- 20+ prioritized action items
- Sorted by impact and effort
- Each with estimated hours and step-by-step instructions

---

## Step 4: Take Action

### Option 1: Create Tasks
1. Find a high-priority recommendation
2. Click **"Create Task"**
3. Task is added to your CRM with all details pre-filled
4. Assign to team member
5. Track completion

### Option 2: Dive Deeper
1. Click **"View Details"** on any category score
2. See detailed metrics and evidence
3. Understand exactly what needs fixing
4. Get specific technical guidance

### Option 3: Compare with Competitors
1. Click the **"Competitors"** tab
2. See how you stack up vs 20 local practices
3. Identify your biggest gaps
4. Focus on high-leverage improvements

---

## Step 5: Monitor Progress

### Run Audits Regularly
- **Weekly:** If actively improving
- **Monthly:** For ongoing monitoring
- **Quarterly:** For mature practices

### Track Trends
- Watch your score improve over time
- See which categories are improving
- Get alerts if any scores drop

---

## Tips for Success

### Before Your First Audit:
- ✅ Make sure your practice website URL is in Settings
- ✅ Have your Google Analytics connected (optional but recommended)
- ✅ Have Google Search Console connected (optional but recommended)

### After Your First Audit:
- 📊 Screenshot your initial score (for before/after comparison)
- 🎯 Pick 2-3 high-priority, low-effort recommendations to start
- 📅 Set a goal to re-audit in 30 days
- 👥 Share results with your team

### Common Questions:

**Q: How often should I run audits?**
A: Monthly is ideal. Weekly if you're actively improving.

**Q: Do I need Google Analytics connected?**
A: Not required, but recommended. You'll get more insights with GA4 and GSC connected.

**Q: What if my score is low?**
A: Don't worry! The recommendations tell you exactly how to improve. Start with high-impact, low-effort items.

**Q: Can I compare with competitors?**
A: Yes! The system automatically finds 20 nearby dental practices and compares your scores.
