## 🎨 Design Details

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Indigo 600 → Purple 600 (gradient)
- **Secondary:** Gray scale
- **Success:** Green 600
- **Error:** Red 600
- **Background:** White + subtle gradients

### Typography
- **Headlines:** 2xl-4xl, bold
- **Body:** Base-lg, normal
- **Labels:** sm, medium
- **Colors:** Gray-900 (dark), Gray-600 (medium), Gray-500 (light)

### Spacing
- **Section gaps:** 6-8 (24-32px)
- **Input height:** 12 (48px) - touch-friendly
- **Button height:** 12 (48px)
- **Card padding:** 8 (32px)

### Animations
- **Slide-in:** 300ms cubic-bezier
- **Fade:** 200ms ease-out
- **Hover:** 150ms ease
- **All transitions:** Smooth, professional
