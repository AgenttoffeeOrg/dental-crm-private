# Marketing Audit Walkthrough - Video Script

**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Dental practice owners/managers  
**Tone:** Friendly, professional, clear

---

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00-0:30)

**[Screen: CRM Dashboard]**

**Voiceover:**
"Hi! In this quick video, I'll show you how to use the Marketing Audit feature in your Dental CRM to analyze your practice's online presence and get actionable recommendations to attract more patients."

**[Highlight: Marketing Audit in sidebar]**

"The Marketing Audit runs a comprehensive analysis of your website, Google Business Profile, reviews, and online visibility - then tells you exactly what to improve."

---

## Scene 2: Running Your First Audit (0:30-1:30)

**[Click: Marketing Audit]**

**Voiceover:**
"Let's run our first audit. Simply click on 'Marketing Audit' in the sidebar."

**[Screen: Empty state]**

"If this is your first time, you'll see this welcome screen. Just click 'Run Your First Audit' and the system does the rest."

**[Click: Run Your First Audit button]**

**[Screen: Loading state with progress]**

"The audit takes about 2-3 minutes. It's checking your website speed, analyzing your Google reviews, finding competitors, and much more."

**[Show: Progress indicators]**

"You can stay on this page or navigate away - we'll notify you when it's done."

---

## Scene 3: Understanding Your Score (1:30-2:30)

**[Screen: Dashboard with results]**

**Voiceover:**
"Great! Your audit is complete. Here's your Marketing Health Score - 78 out of 100."

**[Highlight: Composite score]**

"This score combines 5 key areas:"

**[Highlight each category as mentioned]**

"Technical SEO - 85 - Your website is fast and well-indexed.

Local Presence - 72 - Your Google Business Profile and reviews.

Content & Authority - 61 - Your website content and backlinks.

Analytics - 88 - Your tracking setup is excellent.

Conversion UX - 92 - Your booking and contact options are great."

**[Highlight: Percentile rank]**

"You're ranking 5th out of 12 local dental practices - that's 65th percentile. Pretty good, but there's room to improve!"

---

## Scene 4: Acting on Recommendations (2:30-3:30)

**[Scroll to: Recommendations panel]**

**Voiceover:**
"Now for the magic - actionable recommendations. The system found 20 specific things you can do to improve your score."

**[Highlight: Top recommendation]**

"Each recommendation shows you:"

**[Point to badges]**

"Impact - how much this matters.  
Effort - how long it takes.  
Priority - calculated automatically."

**[Click: Show action steps]**

"And best of all - step-by-step instructions! For example, 'Fix 7 Index Coverage Errors' tells you exactly how to do it."

**[Click: Create Task button]**

"With one click, you can create a task in your CRM, assign it to your team, and track completion."

**[Show: Task created toast notification]**

"Task created! Now it's in your workflow."

---

## Scene 5: Comparing with Competitors (3:30-4:00)

**[Click: Competitors tab]**

**Voiceover:**
"Want to see how you stack up? The Competitors tab shows you vs 20 local practices."

**[Show: Comparison table]**

"You can see who's ranking higher, how many reviews they have, and exactly where your gaps are."

**[Highlight: Gap to top 3]**

"You're 14 points behind the top 3. Focus on increasing your reviews and building backlinks to close this gap."

---

## Scene 6: Wrap-Up (4:00-4:30)

**[Screen: Overview dashboard]**

**Voiceover:**
"That's it! With the Marketing Audit, you can:"

**[Bullet points appear]**

"✓ Audit your entire online presence in 3 minutes  
✓ Get a clear, honest score  
✓ See exactly what to improve  
✓ Compare with competitors  
✓ Create tasks and track progress  
✓ Re-audit monthly to see improvements"

**[Final screen: CTA]**

"Run your first audit today and start attracting more patients. Thanks for watching!"

---

## B-Roll Suggestions

- Dashboard overview panning
- Scores animating in
- Clicking through tabs
- Creating tasks
- Mobile view
- Before/after score comparison

---

## Key Messages to Emphasize

1. **It's easy** - One click, 3 minutes
2. **It's comprehensive** - 5 categories, 20+ recommendations
3. **It's actionable** - Specific steps, create tasks
4. **It's competitive** - See vs competitors
5. **It works** - Real Google data, real improvements

---

## Call to Action

"Ready to improve your practice's online presence? Click 'Run Your First Audit' now!"

---

**Recording Notes:**
- Keep energy high and positive
- Use cursor/pointer to guide viewer
- Pause briefly after showing new screens
- Smile in voiceover (people can hear it!)
- End with clear CTA

**Post-Production:**
- Add gentle background music
- Add text overlays for key points
- Speed up loading screens (time-lapse)
- Add thumbnail: "3-Minute Marketing Audit"

---

**Target Platforms:**
- YouTube (unlisted or public)
- In-app help center
- Onboarding emails
- Sales demos
