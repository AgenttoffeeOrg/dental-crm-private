## 🚀 HOW TO USE:

### **Daily Workflow:**
1. Go to `/tasks`
2. Click "Today" tab
3. Click "Start Queue"
4. Work through tasks one by one
5. Complete & Next until done
6. 🎉 Celebrate!

### **Keyboard Power User:**
1. Use `j/k` to navigate
2. Press `x` to complete
3. Press `Enter` to open
4. Press `Cmd+N` for new task

### **Bulk Operations:**
1. Select multiple tasks (checkboxes)
2. Use bulk actions (Complete/Reassign/Reschedule/Delete)
3. Confirm and done!

### **Calendar Planning:**
1. Click "Calendar" view
2. See all tasks by date
3. Click date to create task
4. Visual planning

### **Team Analytics:**
1. Click "Analytics" view
2. See completion rates
3. Track team performance
4. Identify bottlenecks
